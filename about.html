About DOZI

Made by us.
Made yours.

DOZI is a fashion house that works like a design studio. We make the base — a polo, a trouser, a jacket, a shoe — and the parts that change it. You decide how it becomes yours.

The methodCut / Overlap / Shift / Reassemble
01Cut

Every piece is drafted as parts. Sleeves, collars, panels and pockets are cut to come away cleanly.

02Overlap

Panels and straps layer over the base, so contrast is added rather than printed.

03Shift

A cropped jacket becomes a vest. A short becomes a trouser. The fit moves with you.

04Reassemble

Parts move between pieces through three systems: the modular snap, the zip channel and the strap rail.

The leaf is cut
in two.

Our mark is a single leaf, split and shifted. Two parts, one form. It is on every snap, pull and label.
